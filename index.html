Philosophy

How I
think
about
security.

01
Security earns its seat by making the business more capable, not just more protected. The most effective security leaders don't ask for a place at the table — they create value that makes the invitation inevitable.
02
A rough financial estimate of a real risk is more useful than a precise heat map of an abstract one. Quantification isn't about certainty — it's about structured honesty.
03
The goal of governance is to make the right thing the easy thing. If your framework creates friction without reducing risk, it will be bypassed. Design for adoption, not compliance.
04
Metrics that don't change decisions aren't metrics — they're reporting theater. Every metric should have an owner, a target, and a consequence.
